About Toolvexa

Toolvexa builds simple, free calculators that load fast and respect your privacy. Every calculation runs in your browser, so the numbers you type are never sent to us.

Our goal is to give clear answers to everyday money, health and math questions without sign-ups or clutter. Results are estimates for general information and are not financial, medical or legal advice.
