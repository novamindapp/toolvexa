How to calculate a percentage

To find a percentage of a number, multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. For example, 15% of 200 is 200 × 15 ÷ 100 = 30.

To find what percent one number is of another, divide the first by the second and multiply by 100. For example, 45 is 25% of 180.
